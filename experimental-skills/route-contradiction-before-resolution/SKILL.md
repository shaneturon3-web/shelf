# Route Contradiction Before Resolution

When two claims or routes conflict, make a contradiction record before choosing:

- state each route in its strongest form;
- identify evidence, authority, validity conditions, and failure cost;
- mark whether both routes are locally valid;
- identify the owner or authority allowed to select;
- preserve the unresolved alternatives and the reason they cannot coexist.

Return a conditional route, an explicit selection request, or a documented
decision. Do not average incompatible claims, choose the most legible one, or
turn a live conflict into consensus by paraphrase.

Failure condition: if the output hides a valid route, erases its cost, or makes a
selection without an authority path, classify the result as unresolved.
