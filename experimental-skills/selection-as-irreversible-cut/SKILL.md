# Selection as Irreversible Cut

Before a cut, enumerate the alternatives and the future each one preserves or
kills. Record the authority, threshold, timing pressure, uncertainty, and
recovery limit. Execute only an authorized cut and leave a receipt containing:

- surviving route or material;
- discarded route or material;
- rationale and evidence;
- owner and timestamp;
- what later evidence could still overturn the judgment.

Use the same record for deletion, retirement, quarantine, branch selection, and
editorial exclusion. Do not rewrite a successful result as inevitable. If the
choice cannot be made without inventing certainty, preserve the conflict and
route it to the proper authority.

Failure condition: a cut without a visible loss, receipt, or authority path is
not a completed selection; it is an uncontrolled mutation.
